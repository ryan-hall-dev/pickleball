A quieter match day

Big court actions. Waiting time beside each name. A called group shared by hosts stays put until it starts or is explicitly changed.

Variety, without ceremony

The agreed trial limit is at most 2 additional minutes of waiting for variety. Never leave a court empty for variety. Worked examples must establish how to enforce that limit: comparing wait ages alone is not enough. This mockup runs no scheduler.

Still to agree

The final layout and examples for competing court choices, safe Undo and exceptional interrupted games. Court numbers identify matches only.
