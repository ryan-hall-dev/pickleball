## Simpler phone workflow (approved 2026-11-14; implementation authorised 2026-11-15)

The new host workflow follows `docs/designs/simple-social/phone-review.html` and `docs/plans/simple-social-mvp.md`. These supersede the older desktop-first Setup/live layouts for the simpler version. Keep the existing colour/typography tokens and 44 px touch targets. The host enters a court count; live views show Courts/Up next, Waiting/rest and Check-ins. No Rotation settings, saved-setup/reuse controls, game-count estimates, capacity diagram, Pause control, Singles selector or host History/report screen. Ten-minute rests preserve earned waiting; called groups stay fixed. Normal game turnover is Game finished, then Start these four, without repeated confirmation dialogs. Static mockup scenes illustrate layout, not a functioning scheduler.

## Compact phone layout (approved 2026-11-20, `docs/plans/mobile-compact-ui.md`)

Below 1024 px (the `compact:` variant in `globals.css`, scoped to `.host-compact`: host screens and sign-in, never the player check-in page; extended from phones to tablets 2026-11-21 after the 8-court rehearsal), host screens follow Ryan's Design A (`docs/designs/mobile-compact/mobile-live-compact-A.html`); 1024 px and up keep the sizes below. Phones: 52 px sticky top bar (logo 28, social name 16/700, EN/VN, ☰ menu holding Session setup, Live courts, Hosts, the host and Sign out); page padding 12 px, 10 px gaps; cards 16 px radius, 12 px padding; page/session titles 17/700, section headings 16/700; body 14 px; buttons 44 px, 12 px radius, 13 px labels; primary CTA 48 px. Live order on phones is Courts, Waiting, Check-ins, with a jump bar (counts) and a lime + that opens Add player as a bottom sheet. Tablets (768 px up) show courts two per row. A playing court with nothing prepared shows Court options and an outline Prepare next four side by side. A ready court is one blue card ("Ready to start" pill); a free court is one row with Prepare next four on the right; waiting rows are list rows divided by 1 px lines with their buttons on the right. Things drawn under 44 px (EN/VN 32, jump links 38, End 40) keep a 44 px tap area (`tap-area-44`). Wording is unchanged; long names wrap, never truncate.

## Light mode (approved 2026-11-21, `docs/plans/light-mode.md`)

Light is the default on every screen: dark screens were hard to read under bright court lights. The colour tables below are the **dark** palette, unchanged; it is now the `:root[data-theme="dark"]` override in `globals.css`. The light values sit in `@theme`, and `src/app/themeContrast.test.ts` holds them to 7:1 for text and status text and 4.5:1 for secondary text.

- **Status colours as text use the `*-ink` tokens** (`text-lime-ink`, `text-blue-ink`, `text-orange-ink`): deep green, blue and orange in light, and the solids in dark. `lime`, `blue` and `orange` are for fills only, with their `on-*` text.
- **Modal shadow** is the `shadow-modal` token. It is softer in light.
- **Focus ring** is `lime-ink`.
- **The switch:** a moon/sun button (`ThemeSwitch`, `aria-pressed`, "Dark mode") sits before EN | VN in the host header and on sign-in. It is 44 px, or 32 px with `tap-area-44` on compact screens. Each device remembers its choice in `localStorage`, and an inline script in `<head>` applies it before the first paint.
- **The player check-in page is always light** and has no switch.
- **QR codes stay black on white** in both themes.

## Reclub to court (approved 2026-11-26, `docs/plans/reclub-to-court-v1.md`)

Source: `docs/designs/reclub-to-court/review.html` (31 phone screens, all approved by Ryan). Not built yet; light theme and phone only were drawn, so dark mode, tablet and the Vietnamese wording (native check needed) follow the rules above when built.

- **Import:** the host pastes the Reclub text; only Confirmed names are read, the waitlist is left out, `(+1)` adds "Guest of <name>". Every line gets its own UUID. One-word names are accepted. A name listed twice shows as "Name (1)" / "Name (2)" with an optional host note. Pasting again only adds new names and never removes or resets anyone.
- **One page (Ryan, 2026-11-26):** there is no Arrivals page or tab. The arrivals section (paste, Needs you, Not arrived, Arrived, Show QR code) sits on Session setup above Who's playing, and under Check-ins on Live courts. On Setup the walk-in "Check-ins waiting" box shows only while someone is waiting.
- **Up next is automatic (Ryan, 2026-11-26):** a court's next four are lined up as soon as its game starts ("Ask the next four" default 0, shown as "Straight away"; 1-30 delays it). No "Prepare next four", "Call these four" or "Release group" buttons in v2; Change players stays; Undo releases a newer group by itself.
- **Arrivals:** listed players claim their name on their phone and go straight into Ready. Only walk-ins (Approve / Reject) and "That's my name" (Move to new phone / Keep as it is) need the host. The host can check in a player with no phone and can close walk-ins.
- **Player search:** at least 2 letters, at most 5 results, names only, never who has arrived; the one exception is a numbered twin already taken, shown greyed "In use".
- **Wording:** the next group for a court is **"Up next"** everywhere (never "On Deck").
- **Live courts (compact, 5 to 10 courts):** a "Call over" box at the top lists who to find and where; one short card per court (name, status pill, players on one line, Game finished or Start game); its Up next group sits inside the card as one line of names with ✓ or !. The host can also tap Start game.
- **Player states:** Ready (waiting time, Play with a friend, Take a break, I'm leaving) → Up next (blue, "I'm ready" / "Need a rest", with "Give up this spot?") → Confirmed, wait → Head to court (any of the four taps Game started) → Playing → optional score. Change, moved court, friend request, break and score-recorded states as drawn.

## Principles

- Dark, high-contrast, glanceable from a distance: hosts use this standing at the courts, often on a tablet.
- Status is never colour alone: always pair colour with text (and an icon where there's room).
- Touch targets at least 44px. Primary actions are large (52-64px high).
- Lime means "playing / go / primary action". Blue means "next / secondary action". Orange means "attention".
- Desktop-first, wraps cleanly to tablet and phone (flex-wrap and auto-fit grids, no fixed widths except modals).
- Numbers are tabular (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) so timers and counts don't jitter.

## Spacing, layout

- Main column: `max-width: 1440px`, centred, padding `24px 28px 48px` (setup 56px bottom); vertical gap 16-18px between sections.
- Card padding: 22px (setup sections), 20px 22px (live sections), 18px (court cards), 16px 18px (stat tiles).
- Gaps in use: 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20, 24.
- Setup: two columns, main `flex: 999 1 640px` and summary aside `flex: 1 1 360px`.
- Live: header card `flex: 2 1 520px` + 2x2 stat grid `flex: 1 1 360px`; court grid `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(300px, 1fr))`; Up next `minmax(240px, 1fr)`; waiting queue `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(190px, 1fr))`; setup member grid `minmax(250px, 1fr)`; setup court tiles `minmax(150px, 1fr)`.

## States

- Hover: links lighten to `#BFDFFF`. No other hover styling is specified in the exports; keep it subtle (slight surface lighten).
- Focus: not shown in the exports. Use a visible 2px focus ring (suggested lime or `#8CC4FF` with offset) on every interactive element. Mark as a decision to confirm.
- Selected: lime tint surface + lime border + check (members, courts), or lime fill (option buttons).
- Disabled: see Disabled button; also `disabled` Singles option.
- Error/attention: orange border and message text, never colour alone (icon `!` plus words).
- Drag: grab cursor on priority and queue rows; keyboard move up/down buttons are required by the plan.
- Loading, empty, pending/failed action states: not designed yet. The plan requires pending and failed states with retry, "Not enough players", "Updated by another host".

## Patterns

- Section card = numbered badge + h2 left, actions right, 16px below to content.
- Forms: label above, hint/error below, auto-fit grid (min 200px), name field spans the full row.
- Overdue court: orange chip, orange bar, orange-edged card, lime "Done" button, "wrap up" text.
- Time always `m:ss` or `h:mm:ss` tabular; estimates labelled "~" or "about"; actuals never.
- Changed elsewhere ("Updated by another host", Setup): orange warning banner (`!` badge + words) "Setup was changed in another tab or by another device. Reload to see the latest — your unsaved changes here will be lost." with an orange outline Reload button; the save status reads "! Not saved: changed elsewhere" and nothing more is saved from that tab.

## Accessibility

- Status = colour + text/icon, always (court chips show time and text; status pills show "Court 3", "Next", "Waiting"; warnings use `!` and words).
- 44px minimum targets (the exports use 36-40px for some compact buttons; build those at 44px on touch).
- Text contrast: all text tokens above are on dark surfaces; verify AA for `#9AA8B6` on `#182028` and `#7D8B99` placeholder during M9.
- Semantics used in the exports: `aria-pressed` on toggle buttons, `role="switch"` for toggles, `role="dialog" aria-modal` for the confirm modal, `aria-current="page"`, `aria-label` on icon buttons and the ring, `aria-invalid` + `aria-describedby` on invalid inputs, labelled landmarks (`aria-labelledby` on sections).
- Drag-and-drop needs keyboard and screen-reader alternatives (`@dnd-kit` plus move up/down buttons).
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; no animation is specified in the exports.
